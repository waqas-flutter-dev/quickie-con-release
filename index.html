Frequently asked questions

How does QuickieCon work?

Devices on the same Wi-Fi network connect directly to each other. One device creates a group and the others join it; files, music and messages then travel over your local network. Nothing is uploaded to any cloud or server.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. QuickieCon works entirely offline. As long as your devices share a Wi-Fi network, even a router with no internet or a phone's hotspot, it works.

How does Group Chat work?

Create or join a chat group and everyone in it can message each other in real time. It's one group conversation, so every message is seen by everyone in the group. Messages aren't saved: people who join later don't see earlier messages, and the chat disappears when the session ends.

What can I share?

Photos, videos, documents and any other file, plus contact cards. On Android you can also share an installed app. Send to everyone in the group at once, or to one person.

What is Music Sync?

One person hosts and picks tracks from their music library; everyone in the group hears the same song at the same time, so the whole room plays as one.

Is it private?

Your files, music and messages travel directly between devices and never pass through our servers. There's no account and no cloud copy. Local transfers are not encrypted, so only share on Wi-Fi networks and with people you trust. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is it free?

Yes. QuickieCon is free to download and use, and there's no account to create.
